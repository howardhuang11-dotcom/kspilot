## Runtime mod dependency: Kerbal Reusability Expansion

The grid-fin variants use the parts "Grid Fin M" and "Grid Fin M Titanium" of the Kerbal Reusability Expansion (KRE)
mod, tested with version 2.9.3. KRE is not included in this repository. It is distributed under the MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2020 EmbersArc (original author); the version used here is the community-maintained continuation at
<https://github.com/TundraMods/Kerbal-Reusability-Expansion> (formerly damonvv/Kerbal-Reusability-Expansion; also on SpaceDock).

## Removed from the public release

Craft files from upstream repositories that state no licence, or whose licence could not be verified, are not
distributed. The tooling does not depend on them.
